## Evidence and failure discipline

The plan maps cases to existing owners and includes files, dependencies, commands, review and expected result. `IMPLEMENTED` is not `APPROVED_LOCAL`; scoped approval is not assembled/release approval. Static review/hash/JSON validity, fixture execution, real loopback services, hosted observations and device/human observations stay distinguishable. Full-suite failure is investigated before scope decision; a narrow pass does not waive a broad failure. New evidence has exact candidate/toolchain/environment/argv/exit/hash/portable locator. Missing/secret-excluded evidence remains missing; never reconstruct a past verdict or historical TDD RED.

DoR/DoD are defined once in S18 and applied by the current plan. Acceptance of current written documents is not permission for hosted changes/APK/signing/publication; existing G1–G4 remain. No new paid service, Firebase runtime, dark theme or cross-congregation transfer is introduced by a skill checklist. The older dark-mode obligation lacks a current explicit support disposition and is recorded unresolved under L0; absent implementation is not proof of approved rejection. The earlier 44px target is satisfied by the current 48px minimum.
